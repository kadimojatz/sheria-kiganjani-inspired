# Sheria Connect

Build a bilingual (English / Kiswahili) legal tech consultation and booking platform inspired by Sheria Kiganjani.

Key features to include:
1. Header & Navigation: Clean, professional header with logo, language toggle (English / Kiswahili), links to Services, Find a Lawyer, Q&A / Resources, and an urgent "Consult Now via WhatsApp" button.
2. Hero Section: Trustworthy, authoritative legal tech design with clear tagline, statistics (verified advocates, cases handled, client satisfaction), and quick-action buttons for booking a consultation or chatting directly on WhatsApp.
3. Consultation Service Tiers:
   - Phone / Audio Consultation (quick legal advice)
   - Video Conference Consultation (in-depth case assessment)
   - WhatsApp Consultation (flexible text/voice note guidance)
   - Legal Document Review (contract / deed review before signing)
4. Advocate Directory & Booking Workflow:
   - Search & filter advocates by legal field (Land & Property, Family & Probate/Mirathi, Business & Corporate, Labor & Employment, Criminal Defense), location, and language.
   - Advocate profile cards showing accreditation, years of experience, rating, and consultation rates (in TZS / USD).
   - Multi-step booking modal: select service channel, pick date and time slot, enter brief case details, and choose simulated local payment options (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, Card).
5. Bilingual Legal Q&A Section: Searchable accordion with common legal questions and plain-language answers in Swahili and English (e.g., land ownership, inheritance/mirathi, employment disputes).
6. Testimonials & Trust Indicators: Highlights of advocate confidentiality, Bar Association compliance, operating hours, office location in Dar es Salaam, and direct contact options.
7. Mobile-first, responsive design with polished typography and accessible color palette.
